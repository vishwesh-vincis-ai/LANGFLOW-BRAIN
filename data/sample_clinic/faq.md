# Smile Point Dental — Frequently Asked Questions

## Location
We are at 14, 2nd Avenue, Anna Nagar, Chennai 600040, above the Canara Bank branch.
Parking for two-wheelers is available in front. Car parking is on the side street.

## Opening Hours
Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM and 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM.
Sunday: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM, emergencies only.

## Payment
We accept cash, UPI, and all major debit and credit cards. No-cost EMI is available on braces and aligners through Bajaj Finserv.

## Insurance
We do not bill insurance directly. We give you an itemised invoice so you can claim reimbursement from your insurer.

## Languages
Our doctors speak Tamil, English and Hindi.
