# Smile Point Dental — Treatments and Patient Care

## How many visits does a root canal take
Most root canals are done in 1 or 2 visits of about 60 minutes each. A crown is fitted 1 to 2 weeks later.

## Does a root canal hurt
The tooth is fully numbed with local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure, not pain. Mild soreness for 2 to 3 days afterwards is normal.

## Implant timeline
The implant is placed in one visit. The crown is fitted after 3 to 4 months, once the implant has bonded with the bone.

## Braces and aligner timeline
Braces and aligners usually take 12 to 24 months. Braces need a review visit every 4 to 6 weeks.

## After a tooth extraction
Bite on the gauze for 30 minutes. Do not spit, rinse or use a straw for 24 hours. Eat soft, cold food on the first day. Call us if bleeding does not stop after a few hours.

## Nervous patients
If you are anxious about dental treatment, tell us when you book. We give longer appointments, explain every step before we start, and you can raise your hand to pause at any time. We do not offer general anaesthesia or IV sedation.

## Pregnancy
Check-ups and cleaning are safe during pregnancy, ideally in the second trimester. Tell us if you are pregnant; we avoid X-rays unless they are essential and we choose medicines that are safe in pregnancy.

## Diabetes and heart conditions
Tell us about diabetes, blood pressure, heart conditions or blood thinners before treatment. Please take your usual medicines on the day. For extractions and implants we may ask for a recent blood sugar report.

## Children's first visit
We recommend a child's first visit by their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Children's appointments are kept short and friendly.

## How often should I get a check-up
We recommend a check-up and cleaning every 6 months.

## Bleeding gums
Bleeding gums are usually an early sign of gum disease. A scaling session and better brushing often fix it. Book a check-up if bleeding lasts more than a week.
