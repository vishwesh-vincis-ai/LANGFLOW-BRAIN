# Smile Point Dental — Booking Policies

## Booking
Appointments can be booked by phone, WhatsApp or walk-in. We confirm every booking on WhatsApp.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment. You can reschedule twice at no charge.

## No-shows
If you miss an appointment without informing us twice, we ask for a Rs 500 advance for your next booking. The advance is adjusted in your bill.

## Late Arrival
If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move you to the next free slot.

## First Visit
Please bring any previous X-rays and a list of medicines you take. The first visit takes about 30 minutes.
