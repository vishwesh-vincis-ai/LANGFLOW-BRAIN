# Smile Point Dental — Price List (2026)

All prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST. Final cost is confirmed by the doctor after examination.

## Consultation
General consultation: Rs 500. Free if you book a treatment the same day.
Second-opinion consultation (bring your old X-rays and treatment plan): Rs 500.

## X-rays and Scans
Digital X-ray (RVG, single tooth): Rs 300.
Full mouth X-ray (OPG): Rs 700.
3D scan (CBCT) for implants or aligners: Rs 3,000.

## Cleaning
Scaling and polishing: Rs 1,500 per session. Takes about 45 minutes.
Deep cleaning (root planing), per quadrant: Rs 2,500.

## Fillings
Tooth-coloured composite filling: Rs 1,200 per tooth.
Glass ionomer filling (temporary or kids): Rs 800.

## Extractions
Simple tooth extraction: Rs 800.
Wisdom tooth extraction (erupted): Rs 2,500.
Wisdom tooth surgical removal (impacted): Rs 6,000 to Rs 12,000, depending on the X-ray.

## Teeth Whitening
In-clinic laser whitening: Rs 12,000 for one session of 60 minutes.
Take-home whitening kit: Rs 6,500.

## Root Canal
Root canal treatment (front tooth): Rs 5,500.
Root canal treatment (premolar): Rs 6,500.
Root canal treatment (molar): Rs 8,000. Crown is charged separately.
Re-root canal (redo of an old root canal): Rs 10,000.

## Crowns and Bridges
Metal-ceramic crown: Rs 7,000. Zirconia crown: Rs 14,000.
Bridge: priced per unit at the crown rate (a 3-unit zirconia bridge is Rs 42,000).

## Dental Implants
Single implant with crown (Korean system): Rs 30,000.
Single implant with crown (Swiss system, Straumann): Rs 55,000.
Implant price includes the surgery, abutment and crown. The CBCT scan is charged separately.

## Dentures
Complete denture (one jaw): Rs 15,000. Both jaws: Rs 28,000.
Flexible partial denture: Rs 9,000.

## Braces and Aligners
Metal braces: from Rs 35,000 for the full course.
Ceramic braces: from Rs 50,000 for the full course.
Clear aligners: from Rs 90,000. Final price after a 3D scan.

## Kids
Child check-up (under 12): Rs 300. Fluoride application: Rs 800.
Pit and fissure sealant: Rs 700 per tooth.
Baby tooth root canal (pulpectomy): Rs 3,000.
