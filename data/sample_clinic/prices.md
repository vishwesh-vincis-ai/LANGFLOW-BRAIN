# Smile Point Dental — Price List (2026)

## Consultation
General consultation: Rs 500. Free if you book a treatment the same day.

## Cleaning
Scaling and polishing: Rs 1,500 per session. Takes about 45 minutes.

## Teeth Whitening
In-clinic laser whitening: Rs 12,000 for one session of 60 minutes.
Take-home whitening kit: Rs 6,500.

## Root Canal
Root canal treatment (front tooth): Rs 5,500.
Root canal treatment (molar): Rs 8,000. Crown is charged separately.

## Crowns
Metal-ceramic crown: Rs 7,000. Zirconia crown: Rs 14,000.

## Braces and Aligners
Metal braces: from Rs 35,000 for the full course.
Clear aligners: from Rs 90,000. Final price after a 3D scan.

## Kids
Child check-up (under 12): Rs 300. Fluoride application: Rs 800.
